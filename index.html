About

Research grounded in real-world signals and deployment constraints.

My research focuses on advancing foundation models through reinforcement learning, multimodal reasoning, and integration with real-world sensing systems. I am especially interested in improving generalization, robustness, and abstraction when models operate on longitudinal behavioral, sensor, and visual data.

Recent work spans top-down image understanding benchmarks, contactless audio sensing for exercise, multimodal physiological datasets, psychotherapeutic monitoring, and deployable mobile systems for health-related applications.

Research

Current directions

RL-tuned foundation models

Fine-tuning reasoning-oriented language models for robust behavioral and mental health prediction under dataset heterogeneity and distribution shift.

Top-down and multimodal reasoning

Studying how multimodal models reason about top-down imagery, spatial relationships, and semantically structured sensor and behavioral signals.

Real-world sensing systems

Building deployable audio, vision, and mobile systems for health, smart environments, and everyday embodied sensing applications.
