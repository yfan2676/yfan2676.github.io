About

Foundation models for health, grounded in real-world data.

I study LLMs and foundation models for health, with an emphasis on robustness and generalization in real-world settings.

My methods include reinforcement learning, multimodal reasoning, and sensing systems that operate on noisy behavioral, sensor, and visual data.
