About

Research with measurable impact in the physical world.

My work sits at the intersection of sensing, machine learning, and human-centered systems. I am interested in how AI systems can interpret real-world signals such as images, speech, breathing, and environmental audio to support health, wellbeing, and intelligent environments.

Public records currently list my academic path as NYU Computer Science (2019-2023), Columbia Computer Science MS (2023-2025), and Columbia Electrical Engineering PhD (2025-present). This site is a curated summary of that record and selected publications.

Research Themes

Current directions

Vision + LLMs

Top-down visual understanding

Evaluating how vision-language models reason about overhead and spatially structured scenes through new benchmarks and failure analysis.

Mobile Health

Audio-based sensing in the wild

Building contactless, deployable systems that estimate exercise and health signals from everyday mobile devices in noisy environments.

AI for Care

Foundation models for daily functioning

Exploring multimodal AI systems that support screening, interpretation, and intervention in smart-home and psychotherapeutic settings.
