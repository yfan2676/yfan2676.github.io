About

Research grounded in real-world signals and deployment constraints.

I study how AI systems can interpret images, speech, breathing, and environmental audio to support health, wellbeing, and intelligent environments. I am especially interested in models and sensing pipelines that remain useful outside of carefully controlled lab settings.

My recent work spans top-down visual reasoning benchmarks, contactless audio sensing for exercise, multimodal physiological datasets, and foundation-model applications in healthcare-adjacent settings.

Research

Current directions

Multimodal sensing for health

Building audio and biosignal systems that estimate exercise and physiological states from everyday devices and unconstrained environments.

Vision-language model evaluation

Studying how multimodal foundation models reason about top-down imagery, scene structure, and spatial relationships.

Human-centered AI systems

Exploring AI tools for screening, interpretation, and intervention in smart environments with attention to practical deployment and user impact.
