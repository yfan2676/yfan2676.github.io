About

Building robust foundation models for health in the real world.

I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University, advised by Xiaofan Jiang and Xuhai Xu. Before starting my PhD, I earned an MS in Computer Science from Columbia University and a BA in Computer Science from New York University, where I graduated summa cum laude.

My research focuses on LLMs and foundation models for health, especially how to make them more reliable, adaptive, and useful when deployed with noisy multimodal data. I am interested in reinforcement learning, multimodal reasoning, and sensing systems that connect language models with behavioral, physiological, and visual signals in real-world care settings.

More broadly, I aim to build AI systems that can support health and well-being beyond idealized benchmarks: models that personalize to people over time, reason across heterogeneous data sources, and remain trustworthy in the messy environments where they are actually used.
